Data Privacy & Security Statement
In compliance with RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) & ARTA Standards
100% Client-Side Architecture: This application executes entirely within your local browser. No data is uploaded, collected, or stored on external cloud servers or databases.
Zero External Transmission

There are no remote databases or tracking telemetry. All encoded service details, checklists, fees, and employee designations remain strictly on your local device.

Volatile Session Memory

Drafts are held only in temporary tab memory (sessionStorage) to survive accidental page refreshes, and are automatically wiped and released as soon as you close your browser.

Complete Local Custody

Generated PDF reports and exported .json backup files are downloaded directly to your workstation under your exclusive control.

Regulatory Compliant

Designed to support national agencies and LGUs in authoring compliant Citizen's Charters under RA 11032 while safeguarding data integrity and privacy.
